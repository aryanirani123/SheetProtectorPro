Privacy Policy – Sheet Protector Pro

Last updated: November 04, 2025

Overview

Sheet Protector Pro is a Google Sheets add-on that helps you lock formulas, ranges, and sheets. We prioritize your privacy and only access data needed for core functionality.

Data We Access

  • Google Sheet data: Read/write to protect ranges and formulas (only in your active sheet).
  • Your email: To set custom editors for protections.
  • No external storage: All data stays in your Google Drive. We don't store or share it.

Scopes Required

Sheet Protector Pro requests these Google APIs scopes:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

What each does:

  • spreadsheets.currentonly: Read/write your active sheet to apply protections.
  • script.container.ui: Show the sidebar interface.
  • userinfo.email: Get emails for custom editor lists.

What We Don't Do

  • Collect personal data beyond what's needed.
  • Use analytics or tracking.
  • Share data with third parties.
  • Store your sheet content.
